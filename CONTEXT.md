# CardFacts catalog

CardFacts records cited US card terms and their history, so readers can distinguish what applied on a date
from what the catalog knew when a revision was recorded.

## Language

**Earn rule（返现／积分规则）**:
A card's reward rate for eligible spending, including the conditions and spending cap. Cash rewards are
percentages; points and miles are units per dollar.
_Avoid_: credit, reimbursement

**Credit（报销权益）**:
An issuer benefit that reimburses qualifying purchases or delivers a wallet/in-app allowance. Its amount,
period, reset basis and eligibility determine what a cardholder may receive.
_Avoid_: earn rate, cashback rate

**Terms version（条款版本）**:
One set of fee, earn-rule or credit terms with a known or explicitly unknown effective start and an inclusive
end. It describes when the issuer's terms apply.
_Avoid_: revision, verification

**Catalog revision（目录修订）**:
The complete content recorded for one product at a particular time, including its descriptions, citations
and all terms versions. A later correction does not erase what an earlier revision said.
_Avoid_: effective date, benefit refresh

**Effective date（生效日期）**:
The first day issuer terms apply. A stated date comes from an issuer source; a detected date marks an
observed change whose actual start was not stated.
_Avoid_: recorded date, verification date

**Recorded time（收录时间）**:
The time a catalog revision became part of the recorded history. It does not establish when the issuer
introduced the terms.
_Avoid_: effective date

**Verification date（核实日期）**:
The date a source was checked for a specific terms version. Rechecking a value does not reset its effective
date or remove an earlier catalog revision.
_Avoid_: revision date, benefit start
