# Preserve effective terms and complete catalog revisions

We retain effective-dated fee/earn/credit versions and add append-only, complete per-product catalog
revisions. Effective dates answer which issuer terms applied; recorded timestamps answer what the catalog
said at a point in time, including descriptions and later corrections. Full snapshots duplicate a small
amount of structured data, but make wiki-style history available in exported artifacts without requiring
Git or reconstructing a chain of patches. Unknown initial effective dates remain unknown and must be
labeled in date queries rather than being presented as verified historical terms.
